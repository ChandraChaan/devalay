Privacy policy

Applies to the Devalay Android app and its backend service.

The short version

  • You can use Devalay fully without an account.
  • Devalay shows no ads. To understand how the app is used and to measure our own ads for Devalay, it uses Google Firebase Analytics and Meta (Facebook) app events, which collect app activity and device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are used to fix bugs.
  • Devalay also keeps its own anonymous usage statistics (which screens and features are used, and when) so we can improve the app. You can turn this off, or delete what was sent, in Settings → Privacy.
  • We never sell your personal data. Usage and device data is shared with Google and Meta only for the analytics and ad measurement described below.
  • You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Account (optional): email, a hashed password, display name, language, chosen deity and rashi, and your synced favourites.
  • Device identifier for guests: a random ID created by the app, stored only as a keyed hash, used to apply daily limits to AI features.
  • Location (optional): "Use my location" asks Android for your approximate location only when you tap it. The phone picks the nearest city itself; the coordinates are not sent anywhere or kept. The app then downloads that city's Panchang, so the request names the city, just as when you pick a city by hand. Requests are not logged against you. Where the app uses its own backend instead, it sends coarse coordinates (rounded to about 10 km) to compute sunrise and Panchang timings; they are not stored.
  • Purchases: if you subscribe, Google Play handles payment. We store a hashed purchase token, product and expiry to unlock Premium. We never see card or UPI details.
  • Usage counters: anonymous play and download counts per item, not linked to you.

Crash reports, analytics, notifications and updates (Google Firebase)

The Android app uses these Google Firebase services, run by Google under the Firebase privacy terms:

  • Crashlytics: if the app crashes, it sends the error trace, device model, Android version, app version and a random Firebase installation ID so we can fix the bug. No name, email, contacts or location are included.
  • Cloud Messaging: a push token, used to deliver festival greetings and announcements. The app subscribes to an "everyone" topic and one for your app language; we cannot see who you are.
  • Remote Config: the app checks whether an important update is required. This uses the same installation ID.
  • Analytics: counts how many people open the app each day and which screens they visit (for example Home, Calendar or the Gita), with the device model, Android version, app version, approximate country and region (derived from the IP address, which is not stored), a random app instance ID and the Android advertising ID. It does not include your name, email, contacts, precise location, birth details or anything you type. We use it to see how many people use Devalay and to measure our Google Ads campaigns.
  • Authentication, Firestore and Realtime Database (usage statistics and feedback): each install gets a random anonymous ID (no account, name or email). Under that ID the app reports whether it is open right now and which screen is showing; which screens and features are used and how often (for example Panchang, Gita audio, a finished japa round, a share); the app language, app version, where the app was installed from, and the Indian state of the city you picked for Panchang. Actions are fixed names, sometimes with a short id such as a Gita chapter number or a song id; nothing you type is included, and no precise location, contacts, birth details or device identifiers beyond the anonymous ID. Only the Devalay owner can read this, to decide what to improve. The data is stored by Google in Mumbai (Firestore) and Singapore (Realtime Database).
    Turn it off any time in Settings → Privacy → Share usage statistics (this also stops Firebase Analytics). Settings → Privacy → Delete my usage data deletes everything this phone sent and turns sharing off.
  • Feedback: notes you send from Send feedback are stored with the anonymous ID, app language and version so the owner can read them. Please do not include personal details.

You can turn notifications off in Android settings at any time.

Install measurement for our ads (Meta)

Devalay advertises itself on Facebook and Instagram. To see whether those ads lead to installs, the app includes Meta's (Facebook's) app events SDK. When you install and open the app, it sends Meta that the app was installed or opened, together with the Android advertising ID, device model, Android version, app version and the IP address. Meta processes this under its privacy policy. Devalay does not use Facebook Login and sends Meta nothing about what you read, listen to or enter in the app.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).

Videos

Bhakti videos play inside YouTube's own embedded player (privacy-enhanced mode, youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube may process data about your viewing under the Google privacy policy. Devalay does not receive or store your viewing history.

AI features

When AI is enabled, the text you enter for AI Status or a Gita question is sent to Anthropic (Claude) to generate a reply. Do not include personal information you do not want processed. Requests are not used to build a profile of you. When AI is not enabled, everything is generated on our server without third parties.

On your device

Favourites, downloads, settings, japa counts and Mandir preferences are stored locally. Downloads stay in the app's private storage and are removed if you uninstall the app.

  • Your birth star and rashi, the days in My events, birth details you enter for a Jatakam and your pooja samagri checklists stay on your phone. Marriage matching and the Jatakam are worked out on the phone.
  • Contacts (optional): "Import birthdays from contacts" asks for Android's Contacts permission when you tap it, then reads only names with a birthday or anniversary, on the phone. Only the dates you choose are saved in the app. Nothing from your contacts is uploaded. You can turn the permission off in Android settings.
  • Backup files and calendar (.ics) exports are made on your phone and go only where you save or share them.
  • "Read aloud" uses your phone's own text-to-speech voice.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept until you delete your account (Profile → Delete account), which permanently removes it. Usage statistics are kept until you delete them in Settings → Privacy, or until we no longer need them. Operational logs are kept for at most 30 days.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with Argon2. Sessions use short-lived tokens with rotation.

Children

Devalay is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
