# Devalay store and conversion audit, 21 Nov 2026

Release 10403 (1.4.0) live in Production. Play Console, last 28 days: 15 listing visitors, 17 acquisitions,
17 monthly active devices, 7-day retention 3. Too few visitors to measure conversion yet, so the fixes below are
ranked by what usually moves installs and retention for a devotional app in India.

## Ranked fixes

| # | Finding | Fix | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The listing exists only in English (en-IN).** The app speaks 11 languages, but a Hindi, Telugu or Tamil Play user sees English text. | Add Play translations for every supported language (`docs/store/play/<lang>/`). | Installs, high. Search ranks on the listing's language; native text converts far better in tier-2/3 India. |
| 2 | **Every in-app share leaves without a Play link.** The day's Panchang share points to `chandrachaan.github.io`, festival wish cards share an image only, wallpapers say "via Devalay", Gita, Rashifal and song shares carry no link. | One shared footer with the Play link and an install referrer (`utm_source=whatsapp…`), added to every share. | Organic installs, high. WhatsApp forwards are the main free channel for Panchang and festival content. |
| 3 | **Live short description says "Free, no ads"** while Play shows the **"Contains ads"** badge (YouTube's player). "Free" is a price word Play's metadata policy discourages. The live full description is out of date (says 47 cities; no Muhurtham, Jatakam, eclipses, pooja guides). A corrected draft already sits unpublished in the Console. | Publish the corrected text (repo `en-IN/`) with the translations. | Trust and policy safety, medium-high. |
| 4 | **Screenshots are raw screens with no captions**, and the first one is a busy home screen. Store visitors read the first two or three screenshots, not the description. | 8 captioned screenshots from the real 1.4 app, value first: Panchang today, festival calendar, Choghadiya/Rahu Kalam, Muhurtham, your language, widget, pooja guide, Jatakam. Localised captions for Hindi and Telugu at least. | Installs, high. |
| 5 | **Feature graphic is Telugu-only** ("Telugu Panchangam · Annamayya songs") and says "Free · Open source · No ads" (contradicts the ads badge). The app is now pan-India. | New 1024×500 graphic: pan-Indian message, 11 languages, no price or "no ads" claims. | Installs (ads and featured placements use it), medium. |
| 6 | **No store tags** (Store settings → Tags is empty). | Add tags such as Religion & spirituality, Calendar, Astrology & horoscope, Music & audio. | Discovery, medium. |
| 7 | **Review prompt fires on a timer**, 20 s after start once there are 6 opens over 3 days, not after a good moment. | Also ask (same Play limits) right after a positive moment: sharing a card, finishing a japa mala, finishing a pooja guide. | Rating count and stars, medium. |
| 8 | **Share-the-app is buried** in More → bottom card, and its message says "free … no ads". | Keep the More card; add a WhatsApp-first "Share Devalay" entry on the home screen's Panchang card and after onboarding. | Organic installs, medium. |
| 9 | **No analytics or conversion events.** Settings promises "No ads · No analytics · Open source", and the privacy policy and Data safety say the same. | Decision for the owner (see below). Google Ads App campaigns optimising for installs work from Play's own install data without any SDK. In-app action optimisation (e.g. "opened Panchang 3 times") needs Firebase Analytics, which would break the in-app promise and needs Data safety and privacy policy changes. | Ads efficiency, depends on spend. |
| 10 | Icon (gold Om on saffron) is clear and on-genre at small sizes. | No change. | — |

## Smaller notes

- Onboarding: welcome → language → deity → city and rashi, four steps, no sign-up, skippable choices. Good. The
  first home screen shows the deity of the day and today's Panchang; no change needed.
- Permissions are requested in context (location on "Use my location", contacts only on import, `WRITE_SETTINGS`
  only for ringtones). Nothing to fix for conversion.
- Play pre-launch report flags a full-resolution `BitmapFactory` decode in a plugin (`i5.c.b`, obfuscated). Low
  priority.
- Content rating shows 7+ "Mild violence" (Ramayan/Mahabharata). Fine.
- The Console has unsaved "Draft changes" on the default listing. Review them before saving new text, so nothing
  in that draft is lost or published by surprise.
