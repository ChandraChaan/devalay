Devalay is a small temple on your phone for the whole family. It's free, and you don't need to sign up.

🌏 In your language
• English, हिन्दी, বাংলা, मराठी, తెలుగు, தமிழ், ગુજરાતી, ಕನ್ನಡ, മലയാളം, ଓଡ଼ିଆ and ਪੰਜਾਬੀ
• Every screen, festival, Panchang name and greeting card, with aartis and mantras in your own script

🎵 Devotional songs that keep playing on the lock screen
• Aartis, mantras and bhajans: Om Jai Jagdish Hare, the Gayatri Mantra, the Mahamrityunjaya Mantra and more
• Annamacharya keerthanas by M.S. Subbulakshmi, Ramadasu keerthanas by Dr. M. Balamuralikrishna, Bhaja Govindam and Siva Panchakshara Stotram
• A deity for every day: Monday is Shiva, Tuesday Hanuman, Saturday Shani Dev or Venkateswara, and the home screen shows that day's songs
• Sleep timer, and downloads for offline listening

🗓️ An accurate Panchang for your city
• Tithi, nakshatra, yoga and karana, each with its end time
• Good times: Abhijit muhurta, Amrit Kalam and Brahma muhurta
• Times to avoid: Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda, Durmuhurtham, Varjyam and Gulika
• More than 300 cities in India and abroad, every district headquarters in the South and Maharashtra, or tap "Use my location"
• Tamil, Malayalam, Bengali and Odia solar dates, Amanta or Purnimanta months, and your state's public and bank holidays
• Choghadiya, Hora and Gowri Panchangam, with the running period marked, plus moonrise and moonset
• Tarabalam and Chandrabalam for your birth star, and auspicious days such as Guru Pushya
• Home-screen widgets: today's Panchang, tithi, a festival countdown, and the running Choghadiya or Rahu Kalam
• Read the day's Panchang aloud in your language
• Birthdays, anniversaries and remembrance days that repeat by tithi, with reminders
• Marriage matching: Guna Milan out of 36 and the 10 Porutham
• Backup to a file, export festivals to Google Calendar, and import birthdays from your contacts
• A festival calendar with reminders: Diwali, Holi, Navratri, Ganesh Chaturthi, Janmashtami, Maha Shivratri, Ugadi, Sankranti, Bathukamma, Varalakshmi Vratam and more

🌸 Muhurtham, Grahanam and Jatakam
• Shubha Muhurtham for marriage, griha pravesham and a new vehicle, with the good time and lagna for your city
• Solar and lunar eclipses with begin and end times and Sutak
• Your Jatakam (birth chart) in South or North Indian style, worked out on your phone
• Daily, weekly, monthly and yearly Rashifal

🙏 Pooja guides and a thought for the day
• Step-by-step vidhanam with a samagri checklist: daily puja, Satyanarayana Vratam, Varalakshmi Vratam, Vinayaka Chavithi, Lakshmi Puja, Maha Shivaratri, Ekadashi and Pradosham
• A verse a day from Vemana, Thirukkural, Kabir, Tukaram, Sarvajna, Tagore and more, with its meaning

🎬 Bhakti videos
• Official temple videos from TTD (SVBC) in a simple swipe-up feed that elders find easy to use

🪔 Virtual Mandir: light a diya, offer flowers, ring the bell, blow the shankh and do aarti

📖 Read: all 701 verses of the Bhagavad Gita (Sanskrit with English), the Hanuman Chalisa, stotras and mantras, the Ramayan and the Mahabharata, plus "Ask the Gita"

🖼️ Wallpapers and statuses: write a blessing such as "Om Namah Shivaya" on sacred art, then set it as your wallpaper or share it on WhatsApp

📿 Japa mala, ♈ daily Rashifal, 🔔 devotional ringtones

Use the app in any of 11 Indian languages, with light and dark themes in five colours.

Devalay shows no ads of its own. The temple videos play in YouTube's player, so YouTube may show its ads there.

All songs and images are public domain or under open (Creative Commons) licences, and each one is credited in the app.
