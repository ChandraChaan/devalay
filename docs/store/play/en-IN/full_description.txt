Devalay is a small temple for your family, on your phone. Free, no ads, no sign-up.

🎵 Devotional songs that keep playing on the lock screen
• Annamacharya keerthanas by M.S. Subbulakshmi: Brahma Kadigina Paadamu, Jo Achyutananda, Shriman Narayana
• Bhadrachala Ramadasu keerthanas by Dr. M. Balamuralikrishna, and Tyagaraja kritis
• Siva Panchakshara Stotram, Ganesha Pancharatnam, Bhaja Govindam, the Gayatri Mantra and aartis
• A deity for each day: Monday Shiva, Tuesday Hanuman, Saturday Venkateswara Swamy. That day's songs are on the home screen.
• Sleep timer, and downloads for offline listening

🗓️ Telugu Panchangam for your city
• Tithi, vaaram, nakshatram, yogam and karanam, with end times
• Samvatsaram, masam, paksham, ayanam and ruthuvu
• Good times: Abhijit muhurtham, Amrit Kalam and Brahma muhurtham
• Times to avoid: Rahu Kalam, Yamagandam, Durmuhurtham, Varjyam and Gulika
• 47 cities, including Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam, Tirupati and Warangal
• A festival calendar with reminders: Ugadi, Sankranti, Bhogi, Kanuma, Varalakshmi Vratam, Bathukamma, Atla Taddi, Naga Chaturthi, Diwali and more

🎬 Bhakti videos
• Official TTD (SVBC) temple videos in a simple swipe-up feed that elders find easy

🪔 Virtual Mandir: light a diya, offer flowers, ring the bell, blow the shankh, do aarti

📖 The Bhagavad Gita (all 701 verses) and stotras in Telugu script, plus "Ask the Gita"

🖼️ Wallpapers and statuses: write Telugu blessings such as "Om Namah Shivaya" on sacred art, then set it as your wallpaper or share it on WhatsApp

📿 Japa mala, ♈ Rashifal, 🔔 ringtones

Available in Telugu, Hindi and English, with light and dark themes and five colour choices.
