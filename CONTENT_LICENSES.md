# Content licences

Devalay's **code** is MIT-licensed (see `LICENSE`). Bundled **content** keeps its own status, listed here. Every item also carries its licence, attribution and source link in the database, shown in the app next to the item.

Rule for adding content: only public-domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA (or equivalent, e.g. Free Art License) material, or material you own. Record `license`, `attribution` and `source_url` for each item in the admin dashboard. Commercial bhajan recordings, film songs, TV serials and modern translations are **not** usable without a licence from the rights holder.

## Pooja guides (`app/assets/data/pooja_guides.json`)

The steps, item lists and mantra meanings are original writing for Devalay (MIT/CC0), in our own words; no puja book
was copied. The mantras are traditional Sanskrit (public domain) and are taken verbatim from the app's own mantra
library (`backend/app/seed/texts.py`) by `app/tool/pooja/build_guides.py`.

## Original audio (CC0)

Temple bell, ghanti, shankh, Om drone, tanpura, bansuri (raga Bhupali phrase), damru and five ringtones are synthesised from scratch by `backend/scripts/generate_audio.py` and released under CC0.
